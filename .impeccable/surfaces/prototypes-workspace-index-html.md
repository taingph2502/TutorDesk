# Workspace prototype surface brief

Mode: Operate

Audience and job: A serious self-directed learner studies a selected source while moving fluidly among Chat, Quiz, Research, and Visualize without losing evidence context. Memory Proposals and Boardroom Recommendations must be reviewable both where they arise and in one consolidated queue.

Constraints: Desktop-first; English/Vietnamese-ready; source-first rather than chat-first; exact citation and learner-state provenance must never require a hidden secondary journey; demonstration content is synthetic; the prototype is read-only except for in-memory review choices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: TutorDesk is an evidence conservation lab. The active source is the artifact under examination, and every explanation, question, learner-state inference, and recommendation is a reversible layer tied visibly to its origin. It refuses the category-default chat column as the organizing center.

OWN-WORLD: A restrained light-table palette of mineral white, graphite, oxidized green, and accession orange. Hairline measurement rules, archival labels, transparent examination film, and small tabular identifiers form the component language. Corners are clipped or squared; state is shown by material layers and accession marks, not floating gradient cards.

STORY: The learner selects a Workspace and source, studies a passage, invokes a Capability from the shared learning loop, inspects exact Source Claims and learner-state provenance in place, and resolves review items without leaving the evidence. The consolidated queue proves that nothing requiring approval can be forgotten.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Variant B, “Reversible Layers,” is the selected base. It makes the source sheet the largest region; exposes Workspace, Source Vault, and Capability context without a dominant chat rail; overlays transparent capability and proposal layers; includes Variant A's compact Session record for active Source Claims and practice attempts; and shows at least one contextual Memory Proposal plus the consolidated review count. The development prototype may retain the `?variant=` switcher as comparison evidence, but A and C are no longer candidate bases.

FORM: Evidence Conservation Lab, Impeccable pick from direction seed `7404141e`. Signature interaction: inline citation badges remain attached to source text. Hover, click, or keyboard focus opens an in-situ popover over the badge containing the supported claim text, revision state, and Variant C's node provenance chain. Source Claims trace to immutable revision and locator, then content hash; Learner Insights trace from L3 through Contextual Fact L2 to Raw Event L1. Touch tap toggles the popover; Escape and outside click dismiss it; dismissal returns focus to the badge; reduced motion reveals immediately. Provenance never moves to a bottom strip or distant side panel.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Resolved decision: Variant B, “Reversible Layers,” is the base. It incorporates Variant A's compact Session record and Variant C's node provenance chain while keeping provenance immediate at the originating badge.
